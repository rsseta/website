Delete Account

Günlüğüm

You may permanently delete your Günlüğüm account at any time directly from within the application.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Günlüğüm application.
  2. Go to your Profile page.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

Once confirmed, your account will be permanently deleted.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • Your email address
  • Your profile information
  • Your Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token

Data That Will Be Retained

To preserve conversations and the experience of other users, the following content is currently retained:

  • Posts
  • Comments
  • Likes
  • Sent messages

These items are not automatically removed and may remain available within the application.
