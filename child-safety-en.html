Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: July 10, 2026

Günlüğüm is committed to protecting children. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward any form of child sexual abuse, exploitation, or child abuse material (CSAM).

Prohibited Content

The following content is strictly prohibited:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
  • Sexual exploitation involving minors
  • Content promoting child abuse
  • Content encouraging harm to children
  • Any behavior exploiting or endangering minors

Reporting Content

Users can report the following directly within the application:

  • User accounts
  • Posts
  • Comments

All reports are reviewed by the developer. Content violating our policies may be removed, and accounts may be suspended or permanently terminated.

Cooperation with Authorities

Where required by applicable laws, we may report illegal content and related accounts to the appropriate authorities.

Age Requirement

The application should only be used in accordance with its Google Play age rating.
